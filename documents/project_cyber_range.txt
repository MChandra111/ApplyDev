PROJECT: TAMU Cyber Range Lab Automation (Capstone-adjacent, 2024)

Stack: Python, Ansible-style scripts, AWS EC2, bash, documentation

Problem
Cybersecurity lab VMs were provisioned manually, causing inconsistent student environments before exams.

What I did
- Scripted EC2 launch templates and security group baselines for 30 isolated lab instances.
- Documented rollback and snapshot procedures for teaching assistants.
- Added logging and idempotent checks so re-runs did not duplicate resources.

Outcome
Cut lab prep time from ~3 hours to 45 minutes per cohort; fewer ticket escalations during labs.

Keywords: AWS EC2, automation, cybersecurity, Python, infrastructure
